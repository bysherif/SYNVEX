Privacy Policy (English)

This Privacy Policy describes how the Android mobile game SYNVEX (package ID: com.aistudio.basslinesurvivor.kzvqmr) handles, stores, protects, and shares user information. By installing or using SYNVEX, you agree to the practices described below.

Summary: SYNVEX does not require account registration and does not collect personally identifiable information (such as your name, email, phone number, or location). All gameplay progress is stored locally on your device. Optional in-app purchases and VIP subscriptions are processed securely by Google Play Billing.

Data Category Where Processed Purpose
Gameplay Progress & Preferences

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
  • We do not access precise or approximate location (GPS), camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, or device photo/media libraries.
  • We do not serve third-party ads, use Advertising IDs (AD_ID), or embed third-party ad/tracking SDKs.

3. Android Permissions Used

  • INTERNET: Used solely to communicate with Google Play Billing (retrieving live catalog prices, verifying purchases/subscriptions) and opening Google Play Store links when requested by the user.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: Required to process optional one-time in-app purchases and auto-renewing VIP subscriptions through Google Play.

We do not sell, rent, or trade user data to any third party. The only third-party provider category interacting with the app is:

5. Subscriptions & Cancellation

SYNVEX offers optional auto-renewing subscriptions: SYNVEX VIP PASS (Monthly) and SYNVEX ELİT PASS (3-Month). Payment is charged to your Google Play account upon confirmation. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled before the end of the current billing period. 6. Data Security, Retention & Deletion

  • Deletion: Because game data is stored locally on your device, you can permanently delete all app data at any time via Android Settings → Apps → SYNVEX → Storage → Clear Storage, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Android Auto Backup: If enabled in your device settings, Android may back up local preferences to your personal Google Drive backup. You can disable this anytime in Android Settings.
